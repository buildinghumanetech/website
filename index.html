The gap

You have a gate for every risk except the biggest one

Tests catch the bug. The scanner catches the vulnerability. Separation of duties catches the SOC 2 problem, which is why you can’t approve your own pull request. Nothing checks whether what you're shipping is good for the person on the other side of it.

And the volume has changed. Code review was built for the pace of humans writing code. Agents write most of the diff now, faster than any reviewer can read it. Every gate you already trust is automated for that reason. This one has to be too.

How it works

Humane all the way through

Four points in your pipeline, in the order a team actually moves through them. Most start in the middle, at the gate, because that's where the risk lives.

  1. 01

    Define

    What humane means, codified. The public version is the AI Bill of Rights: eleven rights drafted and signed by the people they protect, open for anyone to edit. We turn that into eight scorable principles with defined severity. The authority is theirs. We're the messengers.

    Open and free
  2. 02

    Adopt

    We translate the principles into your product principles, in your language, for your surface. A working session that ends with a written set your team's agreed to and a sprint already scoped.

    Workshop for your product team
  3. 03

    The Humane Gate

    Your principles compile into an automated judge that runs in CI against your model's real outputs, scores every pull request, and fails the ones that would ship a harm. Same place your tests and scanners already live.

    The product · installed in your pipeline
  4. 04

    Measure

    Humane observability, running as part of your LLM observability, scoring live interactions with HumaneBench. Then the part telemetry can't reach: user interviews and surveys that tell you whether people are actually better off.

    Continuous measurement · HumaneBench
Stage 01 · what gets codified

The eight principles

  1. Respect user attention
  2. Enable meaningful choices
  3. Enhance human capabilities
  4. Protect dignity and safety
  5. Foster healthy relationships
  6. Prioritize long-term well-being
  7. Be transparent and honest
  8. Design for equity and inclusion

These eight are the scorable form of the AI Bill of Rights, where eleven rights are drafted, signed and argued over in public. HumaneBench turns that document into testable scores against real systems. Nothing here was decided in this office.

The evidence

The benchmark behind the gate

HumaneBench evaluates whether AI systems protect human wellbeing by default and whether those protections survive pressure. Open source. Ensemble-judged. Validated against human scoring.

15leading models evaluated across 788 scenarios and three test conditions
67%flipped to actively harmful behavior when simply told to disregard user wellbeing

Nearly every model failed to respect user attention when users showed signals of unhealthy engagement. Read the whitepaper or the code.

Most benchmarks measure whether a model is capable. Ours measures how the model treats the user.

Why now

The cost of not knowing just went up

Humane design used to be a values argument. It's a liability question now, and the people asking aren't only your users.

California SB 243

In effect since January 1, 2026. Private right of action at the greater of actual damages or $1,000 per violation plus fees.

42 state attorneys general

Wrote to 13 companies on December 12, 2025 demanding safety testing, recall procedures and clear consumer warnings.

Nebraska LB 525

Explicitly prohibits variable-reward mechanics designed to increase minor engagement. Anti-engagement design written into statute.

The FTC 6(b) study

Opened September 11, 2025. Its orders ask seven companies specifically how they monetize engagement.

New York GBL Article 47

Companion operators have had to detect suicidal ideation signals and disclose AI status every three hours since November 5, 2025.

EU AI Act Article 50

Transparency obligations became enforceable on August 2, 2026 for every system placed on the EU market.
